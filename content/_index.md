I am a fifth-year Integrated MSc Mathematics student at [NISER](https://www.niser.ac.in/), with a minor in computer science. I work on **formal verification** and **automated reasoning**: writing machine-checked proofs in [Lean 4](https://leanprover-community.github.io/) and asking what it takes to trust a piece of software.

Most recently I was a research intern at [FP Launchpad](https://fplaunchpad.org/), IIT Madras, with [Dr. KC Sivaramakrishnan](https://kcsrk.info/), verifying replicated data types for distributed systems. Before that I spent a summer on logic and automated reasoning at [IMSc](https://www.imsc.res.in/) Chennai with [Dr. Meena Mahajan](https://www.imsc.res.in/~meena/).
